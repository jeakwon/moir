| `src/models/rome/layer_stats.py` (largely rewritten for MOIR: self-generation and C0 estimation), `src/models/rome/repr_tools.py`, `src/models/rome/tok_dataset.py`, `src/util/generate.py`, `src/util/logit_lens.py`, `src/util/globals.py`, `src/util/hparams.py` | ROME (Meng et al., 2022), https://github.com/kmeng01/rome, as distributed in EasyEdit | MIT (text A) |

| all of the above, as distributed in EasyEdit | EasyEdit (zjunlp), https://github.com/zjunlp/EasyEdit | MIT (text B) |

All other files (`src/compute_z_batched.py`, `src/evaluate.py`, `configs/`, `hparams/`,
`scripts/`) were written by the MOIR authors. `src/evaluate.py` re-implements the editing
metrics of EasyEdit. `scripts/run_seq_edit.py` contains optional re-implementations of the
RECT (Gu et al., 2024) and PRUNE (Ma et al., 2025) regularizers.

## Tools and data used at run time (not included)

- lm-evaluation-harness (EleutherAI), MIT. human-eval (OpenAI), MIT; the HumanEval problems
  ship with that package.
- CounterFact and the zsRE evaluation split, from the ROME release
  (https://rome.baulab.info/data/dsets/).
- Wikipedia (`wikipedia`, config `20200501.en`, for C_Wiki): CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL.
- Models: allenai/OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct and Qwen/Qwen3-8B (Apache-2.0);
  meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (Llama 3.1 Community License, gated). Text generated by a
  model, such as a self-generated C0 corpus, falls under that model's license.
